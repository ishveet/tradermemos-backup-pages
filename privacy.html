TraderMemos Backup Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Purpose

TraderMemos Backup is a personal-use integration used to back up and restore encrypted TraderMemos data through Google Drive.

Google user data accessed

The application uses Google Drive access limited by the drive.file scope. It may access the content and metadata of Google Drive files created or used with this application as required to perform backup, restore, verification, and deletion operations.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide backup and restore functionality initiated by the application owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling, marketing, or analytics.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials used by rclone are stored locally on the owner's computer. Backup payloads are encrypted with restic before they are stored in Google Drive. TraderMemos Backup does not operate a separate server that collects or stores Google user data.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising, marketing, or unrelated purposes.

Retention and deletion

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. The owner can also revoke the application's Google Account access at any time through Google Account settings.

Contact

Questions about this integration can be directed to the support contact shown on its Google OAuth consent screen.
